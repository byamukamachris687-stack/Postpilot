# Outreach Playbook — AI Social Media Agency (target niche: local restaurants)

I picked **restaurants** as your starting niche. Why: every city has hundreds of them, owners are busy, they already know they "should" post on social but don't, and $250–$500/mo is a no-brainer next to their ad spend. Easy to find on Google Maps + Instagram.

## The offer
"$250/mo — I'll create and schedule 4 posts/week across your Facebook, Instagram & TikTok. On-brand, with photos prompts. Cancel anytime. First week free as a sample."

## Where to find prospects (free)
- Google Maps: search "restaurant" in your city → list of 50 with phone/website
- Instagram: search local hashtags (#yourcityfood, #yourcityeats)
- Facebook groups: local community groups

## Channel 1 — Instagram DM (best conversion)
Find a restaurant that hasn't posted in 2+ weeks (or posts blurry phone pics). DM:

> Hey [Name]! Love what you're doing at [Restaurant]. Noticed your last post was [X weeks] ago — I help local restaurants stay active on IG/TikTok without the owner doing any work. I made 3 sample posts for you (free, no strings): [link to /sample with their name]. Want me to send the full week? Takes 2 min to review.

## Channel 2 — Email (find via website/Google)
Subject: 3 free Instagram posts for [Restaurant Name]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I run a small agency that creates social content for restaurants. I built 3 sample posts for [Restaurant] using AI + your menu vibe — completely free, no catch.
>
> See them here: [link]
>
> If you like them, I can do a full month (4 posts/week, FB+IG+TikTok, scheduled) for $250. First week free to try.
>
> Worth a 10-minute chat this week?
>
> [Your name]

## Channel 3 — In person (highest close rate)
Walk in during slow hours (2-4pm). Bring a printed one-pager with 3 sample posts for THAT restaurant (generate them first via /sample). Say:

> "I made these for you — free. If you want this every week, it's $250/mo. Can I show you?"

Owners respect hustle. Close rate on in-person is 5-10x cold DM.

## Follow-up cadence
- Day 0: initial message
- Day 3: "Did you get a chance to look?"
- Day 7: "Last one from me — happy to revisit later." (creates urgency)
- After that: monthly check-in only.

## 7-day sprint to first client
- **Day 1:** Get Groq free key (console.groq.com) → set in .env → deploy app → test /sample with 5 real restaurants.
- **Day 2-3:** Build a list of 30 restaurants. Generate a sample for each. Send DMs/emails to 10.
- **Day 4-5:** Follow up day-3 prospects. Send to next 10. Visit 3 in person.
- **Day 6-7:** Close first 1-2 clients. Collect $250. Ask for a testimonial + referral.

## Math to $50/day
- 6 clients × $250/mo = $1,500/mo ≈ $50/day. ✓
- Time per client after setup: ~30 min/week (generate + review + schedule). 6 clients = 3 hrs/week.
- Upsell: $500/mo "premium" tier with daily posts + stories + ad creative.

## Scaling later (once you have 5+ clients)
- Raise price to $350/mo for new clients.
- Add the SaaS self-serve tier ($29/mo) for restaurants that want DIY.
- Hire a VA to do the weekly generation (you keep strategy + sales).

## Key rule
Don't build more features until you have 3 paying clients. Features don't pay; clients do.
